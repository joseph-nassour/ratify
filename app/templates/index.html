THE RATIFY PRINCIPLE

Don't ask AI to respect a boundary.
Build one it cannot cross.

01
⌘

Gate the obligation

Ratify stops where a decision changes what the human will be bound to — not at every harmless tool call.

02
#

Bind approval to content

Ratification is minted against a fingerprint of the exact material terms, not a loose session flag.

03
↺

Revoke on edit

Change the fee, signer or clause after approval and the old authorization becomes unusable automatically.

04
⊘

Remove the capability

The agent has no Foxit credential. Even a successful prompt injection cannot make a secret appear in its process.
