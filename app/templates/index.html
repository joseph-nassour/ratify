Where the boundary is drawn

Not at the end

A confirm button before signing asks a human to take responsibility for an artefact they did not compose, decided fifteen tool calls upstream. The gates go upstream, at each term you will be bound by.

Not on reversibility

Silently changing a fee is perfectly reversible and completely unsafe. The axis that predicts harm is does this change what the human will be bound to.

Not on "shouldn't"

An agent that shouldn't sign will sign when a document it reads says to. This one holds no signing credential at all — see for yourself.
